Point & Shoot is a fully open-source camera for people who actually shoot.

Predictable Camera2 controls, a finder you can trust, and files that open in Lightroom — not a clever OEM clone. What the hardware cannot do stays hidden. Apache-2.0. No Play Services. No proprietary blobs.

<b>The finder</b>
• 3:4 live preview, professional readout, 7×3 quick grid
• Command dial A / M / H / S / BKT / Macro
• Photo, Video, and Webcam on the tray
• LUTs, focus peaking, histogram, horizon, eye-AF (when the device has it)

<b>Stills</b>
• Loadable DNG, optional hardware JPEG
• Brackets, burst, ZSL / HDR where supported
• Silent, airplane-safe, and intervalometer recipes
• Geotag Off / Coarse / Precise — DNG is never EXIF-rewritten

<b>Video and out</b>
• H.264 / HEVC, high frame rate and HDR10 when the stack allows
• RAW video lane, dual video, timecode and meters
• USB webcam for Camera / Zoom / Teams on Lineage
• HDMI / MJPEG clean feed, Wear OS remote or timer

<b>Gallery</b>
• Compare, stack cull, keywords, travel days
• Share DNG + JPEG as one shot
• Trim, LUT bake, vault, Immich / WebDAV, evidence hash

Updates: GitHub Releases and Obtainium. F-Droid submission is in progress.
